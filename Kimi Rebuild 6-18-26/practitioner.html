The Broadcast Era · 1992–2006

The Record Does Not Begin at a Freelance Gig

It begins at a network television affiliate in 1992 — directing live broadcasts under FCC regulation, where what goes out over the air is permanent and there are no second takes.

Fourteen years across two stations. KPLC-TV, the NBC affiliate, from 1992 to 1998 — Director, Technical Director, Master Control Operator. Then KVHP-TV FOX 29 from 1998 to 2006 — Head Director, Producer, operations crew lead. Live newscasts, specialty programming, promos. Real-time decisions in regulated environments. The discipline required at that level does not come from a course. It is built under operational pressure, repeatedly, until it becomes instinct.

Carr Multimedia was founded in November 2000 — while still employed at KVHP. That is not incidental. The practice was built before leaving broadcast. The decision was deliberate.

The Methodology Era · 2020–Present

The Forensic Methodology Emerges

Thirty-three years of operational pattern recognition converges into a single system: Business Archaeology. The excavation of institutional signal from the accumulated record of what an enterprise has already proved.

The shift from practitioner to architect was not a pivot. It was a recognition. Three decades of watching enterprises abandon what worked, replace it with what was fashionable, and wonder why their signal faded — the pattern was unmistakable. The methodology was not invented. It was extracted from observation, tested against real enterprise outcomes, and systematised into the four-stage process that now defines LEGAiSEE.

The practitioner does not sell marketing. The practitioner excavates institutional memory, classifies it, grades it against the Hierarchy of Truth, and reconstructs the signal that the enterprise has already earned. That signal is then mounted as authority — not fabricated, not invented, recovered.

The Ethical Boundary

What the Practitioner Does Not Do

The boundary is as specific as the methodology. Clarity about what is excluded defines what is included.

Non-Negotiable Exclusions

The practitioner does not generate copy. The practitioner does not invent claims. The practitioner does not produce content that cannot be verified against the Bedrock. The practitioner does not work with enterprises that have no documented history to excavate. The practitioner does not scale beyond three concurrent engagements. The practitioner does not delegate the excavation to associates. The practitioner does not accept engagements where the principal is not the decision-maker. The practitioner does not work on speculation. The practitioner does not discount the methodology. The practitioner does not compromise the 3-Source Rule. The Bedrock Pledge applies to every engagement without exception.

A Private Conversation

The Conversation Is How the Work Begins

The practitioner does not pitch. The practitioner does not present a deck. The practitioner sits down with the leadership of an enterprise that qualifies — and listens.
