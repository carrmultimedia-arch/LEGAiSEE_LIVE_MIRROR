Before We Begin

What This Is Not — And What It Is

The distinction matters before the commission is chosen, because choosing the wrong service for the wrong reason produces the wrong result. The Architect is direct about this.

This Is Not

A Branding Exercise

We do not create a new visual identity, a new name, or a new personality for your enterprise. If what you need is a fresh start, this is not your engagement.

This Is

A Recovery Operation

We recover the identity you already earned — the one that worked, that produced your best revenue, that the market responded to before the drift. We restore it, document it, and deploy it.

This Is Not

A Marketing Campaign

We do not produce ads, social content, or creative executions. The output of an engagement is a strategic document system and authority architecture — not a campaign.

This Is

A Strategic Doctrine

The deliverable is a governing document — the forensic record of your institutional authority and the precise operational framework for deploying it. It informs every campaign that follows.

This Is Not

A Fast Process

Forensic archaeology is not conducted in 30-day sprints. The depth required to produce a Tier 1 confidence finding does not compress without compromising what it finds.

This Is

A Deliberate Excavation

The timeline is determined by the depth of the dig site — by what the evidence demands. An enterprise with 40 years of history requires more excavation time than one with ten. This is the forensic reality.

The Governing Standard

The Bedrock Pledge

"We do not build on sand. We bedrock." Every engagement is bound by the Bedrock Pledge — a non-negotiable set of operational commitments that govern every finding, every claim, and every word of every deliverable we produce.

"We do not 'create' marketing here. We practice Business Archaeology — the forensic excavation of what once worked, the disciplined documentation of why it worked, and the precise reconstruction of that signal for the market that exists today. Not the market you wish existed. The market that exists."

I Clause One

No Invented Claims

Every claim in every deliverable is either excavated from your verified historical record or synthesized through the Triangulation Protocol from verified sources. Nothing is invented. Nothing is assumed.

II Clause Two

The 3-Source Rule

No significant claim enters the final deliverable without two independent sources minimum — ideally three or more converging. Single-source findings are labeled as such and treated as Tier II at best.

III Clause Three

Uncertainty Is Disclosed

Every finding carries its confidence tier — Verified Primary, Probable, Plausible, or Uncertain. We never present a Tier III finding with Tier I confidence. Speculation is labeled. Gaps are acknowledged.

IV Clause Four

Contradiction Is Documented

When sources conflict, the conflict is mapped and documented — not hidden, not resolved by preference, not smoothed over to produce a cleaner narrative. Contradiction is data.

V Clause Five

No Sand, No Exceptions

We do not build on conjecture, convenience, or the preferred version of history. Every strategic recommendation rests on verified Bedrock evidence — or we wait until it does.

VI Clause Six

Errors Are Corrected

If a finding in our deliverable is later shown to be incorrect, we correct it — without defense, without politics, without charging for the addendum. Accuracy is the only client.

VII Clause Seven

The Work Is Yours

Every deliverable produced under a commission belongs entirely to the client. No proprietary lock-in. No ongoing licensing requirement. The doctrine is yours — to use, to deploy, to pass to your successors.

The Private Record

What Is Asked Before the Commission Begins

These are the questions every serious principal asks before a commission is accepted. They deserve precise answers.

Is this market research?

No. Market research is the study of external opinion — surveys, sentiment, focus groups, consumer perception data. What we do is the opposite. We excavate what your business has already proved inside its own record. We do not ask the market what it thinks. We extract what your history documents. The distinction is not semantic — it determines what kind of evidence you end up with.

Do you require access to our archives?

We work with what exists — and we work forensically. Incomplete records are not a barrier; they are the condition under which most excavations begin. The absence of a clean archive is precisely why the forensic discipline matters. We do not cherry-pick the cleanest story. We recover what can be recovered and document what the evidence actually supports.

Is artificial intelligence involved in the excavation?

AI tools are used as instruments — never as the authority. The forensic judgment, the excavation decisions, the signal architecture, and the final record are the product of human analysis. AI accelerates the processing of large institutional records; it does not determine what those records mean or what they prove. Every Exhibit is authored, reviewed, and delivered by a practitioner — not generated.

How is confidentiality maintained?

Every commission operates under a mutual non-disclosure agreement executed before Phase 0 begins. Client records, institutional findings, and proprietary signal architecture are never disclosed, referenced in public work, or used in any capacity outside the commissioned engagement. The private record stays private.

How long does an engagement take?

Project Exhibits run on fixed timelines established at commission acceptance — ranging from three weeks (Exhibit A) to twenty weeks (Exhibit F). Retainer Exhibits operate on monthly or quarterly cycles with no fixed end date. Phase 0 is always the first step, regardless of Exhibit selected. Timelines are not estimates — they are commitments.

Who do you accept commissions from?

Principals only — founders, owners, and executive leadership with the authority to act on what the record reveals. We do not work with intermediaries, resellers, or organisations that require committee approval before acting on forensic findings. The work requires access and authority in equal measure.

Questions not answered here belong in the Shallow Dig brief — where the record, not the FAQ, provides the answer.
