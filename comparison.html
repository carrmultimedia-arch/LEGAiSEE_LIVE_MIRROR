The Archaeological
Parallel

You found it. Most visitors don't get this far. What follows is the complete ten-phase mapping of real archaeological science against the LEGAiSEE methodology — excavated side by side, layer by layer, in the same order the discipline demands.

Descend
◆

Every established business sits atop decades of buried evidence — campaigns that worked, eras that defined them, authority they once commanded and quietly forgot. The LEGAiSEE process mirrors archaeological science, phase by phase: discovery, excavation, conservation, interpretation, and finally — display. The metaphor holds because both disciplines deal with the same fundamental act: recovering value from what time has buried.
