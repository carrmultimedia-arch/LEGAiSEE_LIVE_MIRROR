Era One · 1992–2006

The Signal Era

Lake Charles, Louisiana · Network Television · The Foundation

The record does not begin at a freelance gig or a production house. It begins at a network television affiliate in 1992 — directing live broadcasts under FCC regulation, where what goes out over the air is permanent and there are no second takes.

Fourteen years across two stations. KPLC-TV, the NBC affiliate, from 1992 to 1998 — Director, Technical Director, Master Control Operator. Then KVHP-TV FOX 29 from 1998 to 2006 — Head Director, Producer, operations crew lead. Live newscasts, specialty programming, promos. Real-time decisions in regulated environments. The discipline required at that level does not come from a course. It is built under operational pressure, repeatedly, until it becomes instinct.

Carr Multimedia was founded in November 2000 — while still employed at KVHP. That is not incidental. The practice was built before leaving broadcast. The decision was deliberate.

Ethical Framework

Before there was a methodology, there was a document. It was published in the practitioner's official institutional values materials — predating LEGAiSEE by at least a decade. It reads: "A Scout is Trustworthy, Loyal, Helpful, Friendly, Courteous, Kind, Obedient, Cheerful, Thrifty, Brave, Clean and Reverent. Once a Scout, Always a Scout."

That is not a mission statement. It is a documented ethical orientation. It matters here because the methodology requires something that cannot be extracted from process documentation: the willingness to report what the record actually shows, not what the client would prefer it to show.

Business Archaeology does not cherry-pick the cleanest story. It does not suppress an abandoned asset because the client is embarrassed by it.
