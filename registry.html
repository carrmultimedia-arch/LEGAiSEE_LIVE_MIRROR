Phase 0 · The Shallow Dig

Before the commission begins,
the record is assessed.

Every engagement — regardless of Exhibit — begins here. Phase 0 is not an intake form. It is the first act of the excavation.

This is not a lead magnet.

There is no white paper. No downloadable framework. No automated sequence that delivers a generic assessment and a sales call. What follows is a genuine forensic pass across your institutional record — conducted by a practitioner, delivered as a written brief, and used as the foundation of every subsequent Exhibit.

The Shallow Dig surfaces four things.

Your Estimated Golden Era

The period in your institutional history during which the business operated closest to its highest potential — when performance, positioning, and market conditions aligned in a way that has not been fully recovered or replicated since.

One Abandoned Asset

A capability, relationship, method, positioning, or competitive advantage that existed in your record and was discontinued — not because it failed, but because the business moved on, restructured, or simply stopped paying attention.

The Competitive Gap

The distance between what your business has documented and what your primary competitors are able to prove. The Competitive Gap is not a marketing opportunity — it is a forensic measurement. Where your record is deeper, your position is structurally stronger.

One Undeniable Inflection Point

A moment in the institutional timeline where the business changed direction, absorbed a significant event, or made a decision whose consequences are still present in the current operation. Located and documented in the Shallow Dig brief — not as history, but as active evidence.

This is not a lead magnet.
It is the first page of the permanent record.

The Phase 0 brief is a written document — specific to your institution, produced by a practitioner, and delivered within the timeframe established at commission acceptance. There is no upsell embedded in it. There is no pitch. There is a forensic assessment of what your record contains and what it is capable of supporting.

If the brief reveals that a deeper Exhibit is warranted, that determination will be stated plainly and the appropriate Exhibit will be proposed. The Shallow Dig serves the record — not the engagement pipeline.

The Governing Standard

The Confidentiality Protocol

Everything that enters the Registry — and everything that is produced by the excavation — is governed by a confidentiality standard that is not negotiable, not selectively applied, and not subject to revision at the convenience of any party.

◆
Your Submission Is Private
Every credential submitted to the Registry is read by the Architect only. No team reviews your submission. No system flags it. No algorithm scores it. One human being reads what you wrote and decides whether to open the door.
◆
The Excavation Is Classified
Every finding produced by the engagement is classified as a private deliverable. It is not shared with other clients. It is not used as a case study. The Private Archaeology Protocol extends this standard to all Class VIII evidence.
◆
No Case Studies. Ever.
There are no case studies on a public website. No portfolio pages. No testimonials attributed to named enterprises without specific authorization. The work is private. The results are documented in boardrooms, not blog posts.
